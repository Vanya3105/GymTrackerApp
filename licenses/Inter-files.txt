The fonts in app/src/main/res/font/inter_*.ttf are fixed instances of Inter's variable font
(Inter 4, SIL Open Font License 1.1, see Inter-OFL.txt): text at optical size 14 (weights 400,
500, 600, 700) and display at optical size 28 (weights 400, 600, 700, 800). They were made with
fontTools (varLib.instancer, then subset to Latin, Latin Extended, Cyrillic, Greek, punctuation,
arrows and math symbols, keeping the ccmp, locl, calt, liga, kern, mark, mkmk, tnum and case
features). They stay under the same license.

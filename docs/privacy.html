GymTracker Privacy Policy

Effective 31 October 2026

GymTracker is an Android app (with a Galaxy Watch / Wear OS companion) for logging workouts, made by Vanya Evtushenko. This page explains what the app does with your data.

In short: your data stays on your phone. GymTracker has no account of its own, no servers, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it never sells or shares your data. The only time data leaves your phone is when you turn on Google Drive backup, Health Connect, the watch app, or share or export something.

What the app stores

Everything you enter: workouts, exercises, sets, weights and reps, routines and programs, personal records, body weight, notes, settings, and photos you attach to a workout. It is kept in the app's private storage on your phone, which other apps can't read.

Google Drive backup (optional)

If you choose "Sign in with Google", the app asks Google for two permissions:

  • Its own app-data folder in your Google Drive (drive.appdata). Backups go into a hidden folder that only GymTracker can use. The app can't see or change any of your other Drive files.
  • Your email address (userinfo.email), only to show which account you're signed in with. It is stored on your phone.

Backups go straight from your phone to your own Google Drive. The developer has no access to them. A backup contains your workout data and settings. Attached photos are not uploaded.

To stop: sign out in the app (Profile → Backups). To delete the backups too: in Google Drive on the web, open Settings → Manage apps → GymTracker → Options → Delete hidden app data. Google's own handling of your Drive is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

Other backups and imports

You can save backups to a folder or file you choose, or import data from Strong or Hevy export files. The app only reads and writes the files and folders you pick.

Android's own device backup may also include the app's data, depending on your phone's backup settings. This is handled by Android and your Google account, not by GymTracker.

Health Connect (optional)

If you connect Health Connect, GymTracker can:

  • Write your finished workouts (exercise sessions) and the body weight you log
  • Read body weight, to keep it in sync with the app
  • Read heart rate during a workout (for example from a Galaxy Watch through Samsung Health), to show it in the workout

Data read from Health Connect is only used to show it to you inside the app. It is never shared, sold, sent to the developer, or used for advertising. You choose exactly what to allow, and you can change it at any time in Health Connect's settings.

Galaxy Watch / Wear OS app

The phone and watch apps sync the current workout and the buttons you press (such as finishing a set) directly with each other, using Google Play services on your own devices. This data is not sent to the developer.

Camera and photos

The camera and photo picker are only used when you attach a photo to a workout. The photo is copied into the app's private storage and stays on your phone.

Sharing

When you tap Share (for example a workout summary image), the app creates the image on your phone and hands it to the app you pick. Nothing is shared unless you do this.

Notifications and alarms

Rest timer notifications, alarms and vibrations are created on your phone. No data is sent anywhere for them.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android settings, deletes everything stored on your phone. Google Drive backups can be deleted as described above. Health Connect data can be managed or deleted in Health Connect.

Children

GymTracker is not directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect anything from them. The app doesn't collect personal data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
